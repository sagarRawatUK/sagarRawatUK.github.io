BillOne Privacy Policy

BillOne is designed around local-first business data handling. Invoices, stock records and customer data stay on the device by default, and cloud sync is opt-in and account-scoped.

We do not sell business data. Where cloud sync is enabled, access is restricted by the app's account model and privacy controls to support secure, scoped data sharing.
