Breedly FAQ

Breedly is designed for responsible pet owners to find compatible breeding matches with transparent scoring, health checks and structured requests.

Unsafe matches such as close relatives, inactive breeding windows or incompatible health conditions are blocked to encourage safe and responsible decisions.
