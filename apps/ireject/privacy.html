iReject Privacy Policy

iReject is designed around privacy and emotional safety. Core recovery data such as journals, streaks and saved anchors is kept local wherever possible, and we avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

We do not sell user data. If cloud sync or AI features are enabled, their use is limited to the specific service required to provide those features and remains subject to the product's privacy controls.
