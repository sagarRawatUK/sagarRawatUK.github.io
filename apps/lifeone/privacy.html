LifeOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

LifeOne is a local-first habit tracker. Your habits, streaks and progress are stored on your device. We do not require an account to use the app.

Information we handle

The app stores the habits you create and your check-in history locally. Optional reminders use your device's notification system.

What we do not collect

We do not sell your data and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Google Play

If you install via Google Play, standard Google Play services may collect diagnostic data as described in Google's own policies.
