MemoOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

MemoOne is built to be private by default. Audio recording and transcription happen on your device. We do not require an account and we do not upload your recordings to our servers.

Information we handle

Recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored locally on your device. The app requests microphone access solely to record audio, and storage access to save your files.

What we do not collect

We do not sell your data, we do not run third-party advertising trackers, and we cannot access the contents of your recordings.

Sharing

Content is only shared when you explicitly choose to export or share it using your device's share options.
