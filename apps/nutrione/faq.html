NutriOne FAQ

NutriOne is an AI-powered calorie and nutrition tracker. Snap a photo of your meal to get calories and macros in seconds, scan barcodes, track your weight and exercise, and cook healthy recipes built around your goals.

Take or choose a photo of your meal. NutriOne identifies the foods on the plate and estimates the portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat for each one. You can review and edit every item before logging it.

AI estimates are a helpful starting point, but portions and ingredients can be hard to judge from a photo. For the best results, take a clear, well-lit photo from above and adjust the portion if needed.
