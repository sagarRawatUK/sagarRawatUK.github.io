Features

Eat smarter, without the busywork

Everything you need to understand your food and reach your goals.

📸

AI food scanning

Snap a photo of your plate and NutriOne estimates each food's portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat. Review and adjust before you log.

🏷️

Barcode & food search

Scan a packaged food's barcode or search the food database, then set the serving in grams, millilitres or pieces.

🎯

Personal calorie goals

Daily calorie and macro targets calculated from your profile with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — adjust them any time.

⚖️

Weight & progress

Log your weight, see your trend over 7 days to a year and track how close you are to your goal weight.

🍳

Healthy recipes

Recipes picked for your goals, with ingredients, steps, cooking time and nutrition that scales with servings. Log a recipe in one tap.

🔥

Insights, streaks & exercise

Weekly calorie and macro charts, a nutrition score, streaks and achievements — plus exercise logging that can add burned calories to your goal.

Your day at a glance

The home screen shows calories left, what you've eaten and burned, and your protein, carbs and fat for the day — with every meal just below.

  • Calories left, eaten and burned
  • Protein, carbs and fat progress
  • Meal reminders so you never miss a log

Understand your nutrition

See trends instead of single days. NutriOne charts your calories against your target and breaks down your macros over time.

  • 7, 30 and 90 day views
  • Daily average vs. target
  • Macro trends and calorie breakdown
