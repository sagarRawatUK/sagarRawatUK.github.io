NutriOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information NutriOne collects, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have. NutriOne is provided by MYX ("we", "us"), which is responsible for your information.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and an account identifier.
  • Your name, if you provide it or share it through Sign in with Apple or Google. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email address.
  • Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.

Profile and health information you enter

  • Date of birth, gender, height, current, starting and target weight, activity level, weight goal and nutrition targets.
  • Your answers to onboarding questions (for example what makes tracking hard for you), your preferred units and any referral code you enter.

Tracking data

  • Foods and meals you log, including calories and nutrients, saved foods, saved recipes, exercise sessions and weight entries.

Food photos

  • When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to our servers and to our AI provider to estimate its nutrition. We do not keep the photo on our servers after the estimate is returned. A copy stays on your device so it can be shown next to your log entry, and it is deleted when you log out or delete your account.

Usage and device information

  • App events such as "food logged" or "scan completed", with non-identifying details like the meal type. These events never include food names, photos, your name or your email.
  • Crash and error reports, which include technical details such as the device model, operating system version and the app state at the time of the error.
  • The number of AI scans you make each day, so we can apply the free plan limit.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: calculating your calorie and macro targets, storing your logs, and showing your progress, insights and recommendations.
  • To sync your data across your devices and restore it when you sign in.
  • To estimate the nutrition of food photos you choose to scan, and to look up products by barcode.
  • To send the reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and enforce plan limits.
  • To understand how the app is used and fix problems, using usage events and crash reports.
  • To contact you about your account, and to send tips and offers only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your health information for any purpose other than providing NutriOne to you.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Contract: to provide the features you ask for, including your account, sync and AI scanning.
  • Explicit consent: for health information such as your weight, body measurements and food intake. You give this consent by entering the information, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting it or deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, understand usage and fix crashes, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: for marketing messages.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under contract:

  • Supabase: hosting, database and account authentication.
  • Anthropic: AI analysis of the food photos you scan. Photos are sent without your name or email. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models; it may keep it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting. We configure it not to collect personal details such as your name, email or IP-based identity.
  • Apple and Google: if you choose to sign in with them, and to deliver the app and process any purchases through their stores.
  • Open Food Facts: when you look up a barcode, only the barcode number is sent. No personal information is included.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep information

  • Your account and tracking data are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your account and all synced data are deleted from our database straight away. Copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within a limited period.
  • Usage events and crash reports are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the app, and are deleted with your account.
  • Food photos are not stored on our servers.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this in the app:

  • Edit your details in Profile › Personal Details, Goals and Nutrition Targets.
  • Export your data in Profile › Settings › Export Data.
  • Delete your account and data in Profile › Settings › Delete Account.
  • Turn reminders on or off in Profile › Settings, and manage notification and camera permissions in your device settings.

For anything else, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to provide the app.

8. Children

NutriOne is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or older if the law where you live requires it. We do not knowingly collect information from children under that age; if you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit, your sign-in session is stored in your device's secure storage, and our database restricts each account to its own data. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach where the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will show the new date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app before they take effect.
