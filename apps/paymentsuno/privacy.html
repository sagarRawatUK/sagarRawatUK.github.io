PaymentSuno Privacy Policy

PaymentSuno processes payment notifications entirely on your device. Notification access is required so the app can detect incoming payment notifications from the payment apps you choose. It reads only what is needed to detect and announce a payment — the amount, payer name if shown, payment app and transaction ID.

Payment data stays on your device and is never uploaded to a server. Notification content is never collected for advertising or analytics, and payment details are never shared with advertisers. Logs never contain full transaction IDs, account numbers or other sensitive payment details.

PaymentSuno never reads UPI PINs, banking credentials, OTPs or passwords, and never initiates, approves or modifies any payment.

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and collect usage data to show and measure ads. Where required, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time from Settings. Premium removes ads. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat.
