QRone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

QRone works entirely on your device. Scanning and QR generation do not require an account or an internet connection.

Information we handle

The app requests camera access to scan codes. Scan results are stored locally in your history on the device.

What we do not collect

We do not upload your scans, we do not sell data, and we do not use advertising trackers.
