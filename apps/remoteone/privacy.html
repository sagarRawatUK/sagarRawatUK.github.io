RemoteOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

RemoteOne communicates with your TV over your local Wi-Fi network. It does not require an account and does not route your commands through external servers.

Information we handle

The app discovers devices on your local network and sends control commands to the TV you select. This happens on your network only.

What we do not collect

We do not sell data and we do not use advertising trackers. No viewing data is collected by MYX.
