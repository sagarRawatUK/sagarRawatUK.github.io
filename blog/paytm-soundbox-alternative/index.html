Why shops look for a soundbox alternative

Soundboxes changed Indian counters. Instead of asking customers to show a payment screen, or trusting a screenshot, you hear "Payment received" and move on. Paytm's Soundbox made the idea mainstream, and PhonePe and Google Pay followed with their own devices.

The device is great at what it does. But shopkeepers run into the same few problems:

  • It's another monthly bill. Soundboxes usually come with a device or setup fee and a monthly rental. Reported rentals are commonly around ₹100 to ₹150 a month, depending on the plan and any offers.
  • One box per provider. A Paytm Soundbox announces payments made to your Paytm merchant QR. If you also display a PhonePe or bank QR, those payments need their own box, which is why some counters have two or three of them.
  • Another device to look after. It needs charging, a working SIM and a spot on the counter.

What PaymentSuno does differently

Every UPI app, one voice

PaymentSuno listens for the payment notifications your UPI and bank apps already post: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank apps like SBI, HDFC, ICICI and Axis. When money arrives, it reads it out, like "Payment received, ₹500". You can switch each source on or off, so you only hear the apps you use for business.

Your language, your sound

Choose from Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati and Punjabi, with a male or female voice where your phone supports it. Adjust volume, speed and pitch, and pick an announcement style: voice only, a soft ding first, or a bright bell first. A one-tap Play Test Sound checks your setup.

Closing-time totals without opening five apps

Every announced payment is saved to your history. See today's total, the number of payments and the average, switch between daily, weekly, monthly or custom ranges, and search by name or app. That's one place to check the day's UPI takings, whichever app they came through.

Screenshot-proof by design

PaymentSuno only speaks when a real payment notification arrives on your phone. A customer flashing a fake payment screen at you won't trigger an announcement. It also filters duplicate notifications, so one payment means one announcement.

Private, and it can't touch your money

Payments are detected and stored on your phone, and your payment messages aren't used for advertising. PaymentSuno never reads UPI PINs, OTPs or passwords, and it can't send, approve or change a payment.

Free to run

The soundbox is free with ads. PaymentSuno Premium is optional, removes ads and unlocks extras. There's no rental and nothing to return if you stop using it.

When a soundbox is the better choice

  • The phone isn't at the counter. PaymentSuno can only announce payments that arrive on the phone it's running on. If the owner carries that phone while staff run the counter, a soundbox at the till makes more sense.
  • Your shop is very loud. A purpose-built speaker can be louder than a phone speaker.
  • You don't want to rely on a phone. A soundbox has its own SIM and battery. A phone also needs notification access turned on, and some phones close background apps aggressively unless you allow them to run.

A middle path many shops use: an old Android phone that stays at the counter with your business payment apps signed in and PaymentSuno running. That gives you a dedicated soundbox without the rental.

How to set up PaymentSuno

  1. Install PaymentSuno from Google Play on the phone that receives your payments.
  2. Allow notification access when asked. This is how payments are detected.
  3. Under Settings › Payment Sources, turn on the apps you take payments with.
  4. Under Settings › Sound Settings, pick your language, voice and announcement style.
  5. Turn the soundbox on and tap Play Test Sound. If your phone limits background apps, allow PaymentSuno to run in the background in battery settings.
