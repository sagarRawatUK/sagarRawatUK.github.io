Privacy

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Every MYX app starts from the same question: what's the least data this needs to be great?

  • Local-first by defaultYour habits, scans, payments and notes stay on your phone wherever possible.
  • Minimal permissionsApps ask only for what a feature needs to work — nothing more.
  • Plain-language policiesEach app has its own clear policy covering exactly what's collected and why.
